# Captions

Read `animora-basics` first.

## Adding captions

`add_captions { projectId, assetId, stylePreset? }` builds one caption
item per timeline segment that plays the asset, word-synced to its
transcript.

- `assetId` accepts **either** namespace: the editor asset id from
  `read_project`, or the uploaded asset's uuid. The tool bridges them.
- It needs a **finished transcript**. Uploads transcribe automatically
  in the background; `no_transcript` right after an upload usually
  means "not done yet" — wait and retry once, then tell the user.
- Captions stay in sync across later cuts: each caption item carries its
  segment's source offset.

## Styles

Presets: `tiktok` (huge word-by-word karaoke, the default), `clean`,
`bold`, `boxed`, `karaoke`, `pop`, `pill`, `minimal`.

- Restyle everything at once: `restyle_captions { projectId, stylePreset }`
  — position and timing survive.
- Fine-tune one item: `set_item_property` on the caption item — color,
  `highlightColor`, `fontSize`, `maxLines`, `textTransform`,
  `background`, `karaokeMode` (`color` | `box`), `wordAnimation`
  (`none` | `pop`).

## Picking a preset

Match the product's energy: `clean` or `minimal` for calm B2B tools,
`tiktok` or `bold` for consumer products. When VO was generated by a
plan, captions usually already exist — `read_project` before adding a
second set.
